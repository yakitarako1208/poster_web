Chaekmori

Group3 LATTISM

Jikyeom Bak / Hyojin Kim / Jimin Jeong / Ryusei Hamasaka / Asuma Kakehata

This is a story about a mysterious book stand.

It is a creature that often lives between doors and walls.
Two fairy brothers live where there are many books.
They were born with the mission of managing books.

The older brother, ’낑’, was holding the books straight, but when he dropped something behind him and reached back to pick it up, his body ended up bent backward because his younger brother happened to push from behind.

The younger brother, ‘끙’, is devoted to his mission and looks up to his older brother.
So devoted, in fact, that he doesn’t notice his brother is bent and keeps pushing him.

When there are no books, his push is so strong that his brother nearly falls over.
