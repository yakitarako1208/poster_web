TABI-ME（旅-ME）

ISDW Group3

Problem:
Overtourism in Japan's popular districts causes severe crowding at famous photo spots, while many equally photogenic but lesser known locations remain overlooked due to limited awareness and promotion.

Solution:
A digital stamp rally app that guides tourists to hidden spots in Japan, where they can collect NFC stamps at local shops and earn points for rewards.
