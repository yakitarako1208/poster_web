오우아（おうあ）

Group1 미묘한 오각 관계（微妙な五角関係）

Masaya Okumura / Ryunosuke Sato / Jiwon Seo / Seugmin Lee / Yerim Shon

We designed a product inspired by the rotational principle of Korean vowels.
During a meal with our team, we noticed the difference in the way chopsticks are placed in Japan and Korea, and combined this idea with the principles behind the creation of Hangul to produce a roulette keyring.
