아이와같이（アイワガッチ）

Group5 오공오공

Hwang Jongwon / Lee Juhee / Park Minsik / Koh Matsunaga / Yumeka Miura / Mahiru Okita

Tooth Changing the Behavior of Children Who Don't Like Toothbrush Through Hangeul's 'Lee'.
With a design inspired by the Korean letter "아," "아이와같이" helps children naturally learn letter shapes and meanings through daily brushing.

It encourages interest in letters while promoting good brushing habits.
By supporting children's independent brushing, it brings Korean letters and happy smiles into everyday family life.
