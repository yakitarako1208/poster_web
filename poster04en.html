JA-EUM BLOCK（자음블록）

Group4 Team maru

Ye Eun / Cham Bi / Su Hyeok / Ryota Tsujioka / Masaharu Yamanaka / Miharu Matsusaka

This product is a puzzle that combines Hangul letter pieces to create a tourist attraction, with the aim of creating more family memories.
This product is a puzzle designed for families with children aged 5 to 10 to puzzle assemble together.
It involves children and adults consulting with each other to assemble the puzzle using pieces shaped like Hangul consonants.
